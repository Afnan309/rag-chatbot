# Cancellations, Rescheduling and Refunds

## Rescheduling
Customers can reschedule an appointment for free up to 24 hours before the arrival window. Rescheduling can be done by phone, by replying to the SMS reminder, or from the link in the confirmation email.

## Late cancellation fee
Appointments cancelled less than 24 hours before the arrival window are charged a $49 late cancellation fee. The fee is waived for emergencies, severe weather, or if Northline is running late by more than one hour.

## No-show policy
If the technician arrives and nobody is home within 15 minutes, the visit is marked as a no-show and the $89 diagnostic fee is charged.

## Refunds
If a customer is not satisfied with a repair, they must report it within 30 days. We will first send a technician to correct the issue for free. If we cannot fix it, we refund the labor portion of the invoice. Parts that were installed and working are not refundable.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Maintenance plan cancellation
Comfort Club memberships can be cancelled at any time. If no visit has been used, the customer receives a full refund. If a tune-up has already been performed, the cost of the completed visit is deducted from the refund.
