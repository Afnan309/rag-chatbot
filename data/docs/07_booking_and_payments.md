# Booking and Payments

## How to book
Appointments can be booked online 24/7 through the website, by phone during office hours, or by text message. Online booking shows all open arrival windows for the next 14 days.

## Confirmation and reminders
After booking, the customer receives a confirmation email and SMS. A reminder text is sent the day before the appointment.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, checks and ACH bank transfer. Cash is accepted but technicians cannot give change above $20.

## When payment is due
Payment for repairs is due when the work is completed. For installations, a 30% deposit is required when the proposal is signed, and the remaining balance is due on completion.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed automatically after every visit and include photos of the work, the technician's notes and the warranty terms.

## Permits
For installations and electrical work that require a city permit, Northline pulls the permit on the customer's behalf. Permit fees are listed separately on the proposal.
