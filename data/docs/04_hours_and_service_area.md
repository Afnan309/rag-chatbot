# Business Hours and Service Area

## Office hours
The office is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed on Sunday, but emergency dispatch is available.

## Arrival windows
Appointments are booked in 2-hour arrival windows. The technician sends a text message with their name, photo and live tracking link about 30 minutes before arrival.

## Service area
We serve the Denver metro area, including Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Littleton, Arvada, Westminster, Englewood and Centennial. Homes up to 35 miles from our main office are covered with no travel charge.

## Outside the service area
Customers between 35 and 50 miles from the main office pay a $40 trip charge. We do not accept jobs more than 50 miles away.

## Holidays
The office is closed on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. Emergency service remains available on these days at emergency rates.
