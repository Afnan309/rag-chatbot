# Frequently Asked Questions

## How often should I replace my air filter?
Standard 1-inch filters should be replaced every 1 to 3 months. Homes with pets or allergies should replace them every month. Thicker 4-inch media filters last 6 to 12 months.

## How long does an AC installation take?
Most central air conditioner replacements are completed in one day. Heat pump and full system replacements usually take one to two days.

## Are your technicians licensed and insured?
Yes. All technicians are licensed, background-checked, drug-tested and fully insured.

## Do you offer free estimates?
Estimates for new system installations are free. Repair visits require the $89 diagnostic fee, which is waived if the repair is approved during the visit.

## What brands do you install?
We install Carrier, Trane, Lennox and Rheem equipment, and we service all major brands.

## Why is my water heater making a popping noise?
Popping usually comes from sediment build-up at the bottom of the tank. A water heater flush often fixes it.

## Can a smart thermostat lower my energy bill?
A properly programmed smart thermostat can reduce heating and cooling costs by around 8 to 10 percent per year. We install and configure Ecobee, Nest and Honeywell smart thermostats.
