# Emergency Service

## Availability
Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Call the main number and press 1 to reach the on-call dispatcher.

## What counts as an emergency
- No heat when the outside temperature is below 40°F.
- No cooling when the outside temperature is above 95°F and someone in the home is elderly, an infant, or has a medical condition.
- Burst pipes, major leaks, or sewage backups.
- Burning smell, sparking outlets, or a panel that is hot to the touch.

## Gas leak safety
If you smell gas, leave the home immediately, do not switch lights on or off, and call the gas utility emergency line or 911 from outside. After the utility makes the area safe, Northline can repair the appliance.

## Response time
Our target response time for emergencies is under 2 hours inside the service area.

## Emergency pricing
After-hours emergency visits (weekdays after 7 PM, weekends and holidays) have a $179 emergency dispatch fee instead of the $89 diagnostic fee. Comfort Club members pay the standard rate with no after-hours charge.

## Water shut-off tip
For a burst pipe, turn off the main water shut-off valve, usually located where the water line enters the home near the water heater or in the basement.
