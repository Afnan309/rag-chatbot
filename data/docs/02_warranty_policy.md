# Warranty Policy

## Labor warranty
All repairs come with a 1-year labor warranty. If the same problem returns within 12 months because of our workmanship, we fix it at no charge.

## Installation warranty
New system installations (air conditioners, furnaces, heat pumps and water heaters) include a 10-year parts warranty from the manufacturer and a 2-year labor warranty from Northline. Customers must register the equipment with the manufacturer within 60 days of installation to activate the 10-year parts coverage. Our team registers the equipment for the customer on installation day.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover damage caused by power surges, flooding, fire, pests, or repairs made by another company after our visit. Normal wear items such as filters, belts and fuses are not covered.

## How to make a warranty claim
Call our office or use the online booking form and select "Warranty Service". Have your invoice number ready. A technician will inspect the issue, and if it is covered, there is no diagnostic fee.

## Maintenance requirement
To keep the 2-year labor warranty on new installations valid, the system must receive at least one professional tune-up every 12 months. Members of the Comfort Club maintenance plan meet this requirement automatically.
