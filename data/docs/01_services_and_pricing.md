# Services and Pricing

Northline Home Services provides heating, cooling, plumbing and electrical services for homes and small businesses.

## Diagnostic visit
A standard diagnostic visit costs $89. The diagnostic fee is waived if the customer approves the repair on the same visit. Every technician gives a written, flat-rate quote before any work starts, so the customer always knows the price in advance.

## Heating and cooling (HVAC)
- AC tune-up: $129 per system. Includes refrigerant pressure check, coil cleaning, filter replacement and thermostat calibration.
- Furnace tune-up: $119 per system. Includes burner cleaning, carbon monoxide test and heat exchanger inspection.
- New central air conditioner installation starts at $4,800, including removal of the old unit.
- Heat pump installation starts at $6,200.
- Ductless mini-split installation starts at $3,100 for a single zone.

## Plumbing
- Drain cleaning starts at $149 per drain.
- Water heater flush: $99.
- Tank water heater replacement starts at $1,650. Tankless water heater installation starts at $3,400.
- Leak detection with thermal camera: $199.

## Electrical
- Electrical panel inspection: $139.
- Panel upgrade to 200 amps starts at $2,900.
- EV charger installation (Level 2) starts at $950, not including permit fees.

## Senior and military discount
Seniors over 65, veterans and active military members receive 10% off any repair. The discount cannot be combined with other coupons.

## Financing
Projects over $1,500 qualify for financing with 0% interest for 12 months for approved customers. Applications are handled through our financing partner and take about five minutes.
