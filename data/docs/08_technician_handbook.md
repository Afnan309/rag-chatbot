# Technician Handbook (Internal)

## Uniform and arrival
Technicians wear the company uniform and ID badge at all times. Shoe covers must be worn inside every home. Technicians must arrive inside the scheduled arrival window and text the customer 30 minutes before arrival.

## Safety rules
- Lock out and tag out electrical circuits before working on them.
- Use a combustion analyzer on every gas appliance visit and record the carbon monoxide reading.
- Never enter a crawl space alone without telling dispatch.
- Ladders over 8 feet require a second person.

## Quotes and approvals
Technicians must present a written flat-rate quote from the pricing tablet before starting work. Work above $2,500 requires approval from the service manager.

## Vehicle policy
Company vans may only be used for work. Vans must be restocked every Friday from the warehouse using the restock checklist.

## Time off
Technicians request paid time off through the HR portal at least 14 days in advance. New technicians receive 10 days of paid time off per year, increasing to 15 days after three years.

## Training
Every new technician completes a 2-week ride-along with a senior technician before running calls alone. EPA 608 certification is required to handle refrigerant.
