# Comfort Club Maintenance Plan

The Comfort Club is our yearly maintenance membership for homeowners.

## Price
The membership costs $16 per month or $179 per year when paid annually. Each additional system in the same home costs $6 per month.

## What members get
- Two tune-ups per year: one for cooling in spring and one for heating in fall.
- One free water heater flush per year.
- Priority scheduling: members are booked ahead of non-members, usually within 24 hours.
- 15% discount on all repairs and parts.
- No overtime or weekend charges for members.
- Waived diagnostic fee on every visit.

## Warranty benefit
Comfort Club tune-ups count as the yearly professional maintenance required to keep the installation labor warranty valid.

## How to join
Customers can join online, over the phone, or ask the technician during any visit. Membership benefits start the same day.
